## What Canvas Is

Canvas suits anyone whose best ideas emerge when the thinking and the material sit side by side. Rather than splitting notes, sketches, references, and planning across separate tools, it gathers them into one macOS desktop space, so nothing depends on a cloud-first setup.

Persistence runs through a local file-based vault. That design keeps the workspace tangible: it lives on disk, it can be organized the way you like, and it stays portable. Folders, unlimited pages, and an open canvas layout make it a solid choice for builders, writers, and creative teams working through posts, concepts, or project plans.

## How It Is Used

Treat Canvas as the stage where ideas are collected before they become posts or structured documents.

Workflows people commonly follow:

- Give every project, draft, or content stream its own page
- Place notes, sketches, links, and images directly on the canvas
- Sort related material into folders as a project expands
- Capture a carousel view when you need a polished presentation layout
- Export a page or a JPEG once the work is ready to share or publish

When planning content, it helps to keep research, rough copy, and visual references in a single place. The draft can then develop in one workspace instead of bouncing between tools.

## Configuration

Because the workspace is stored locally, nearly all setup concerns where the vault resides on your machine.

Common ways to organize it:

- Pick a dedicated local folder to serve as the vault
- Separate projects into their own folders so they are easy to find later
- Rely on page-level exports when you want to keep a snapshot of a draft

If your build includes preferences, keep them next to the local workspace so the whole structure remains simple to back up and relocate.

## FAQ

**What sets Canvas apart from cloud-first note apps?**  
Storage is local and file-based. Your workspace sits on disk instead of being built around remote syncing.

**Does it support writing and drawing in the same place?**  
Yes. Text, sketches, images, and links all share the infinite canvas.

**Where does my data live?**  
In a local vault, which keeps workspace data in a file-based structure on your machine.

**How can I share a page or a layout?**  
Use page export to capture a page, or JPEG export for a visual snapshot.

**What if the app will not open?**  
Make sure you downloaded the correct macOS build, check that the vault location has proper file permissions, and confirm there is enough free local storage.
